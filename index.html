What I look for in your data

M-Pesa is one useful input — not the whole product. I combine the records you already have to surface transaction intelligence that is relevant to how your business actually makes money.

Demand rhythm

Peak days, quiet periods, repeat purchase patterns, and product velocity that can guide stock and staffing decisions.

Revenue leakage

Reconciliation gaps, payment mix, discount patterns, cash-flow friction, and the places where sales do not translate cleanly into cash.

Commercial contribution

Which products and customers create useful revenue, where margin may be under pressure, and what deserves a closer look.
